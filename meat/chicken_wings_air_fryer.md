# Chicken wings with air fryer

[Pro Home Cooks - THE BEST Air Fryer Chicken Wing (Ranking 6 Methods)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qKzF0V3ErXA)

| dry mix        | q   | g   |
|----------------|-----|-----|
| garlic powder  | 1t  | 3g  |
| ginger powder  | 1t  | 2g  |
| onion powder   | 1t  | 2g  |
| cumin powder   | 1t  | 2g  |
| smoked paprika | 1t  | 2g  |
| chile powder   | 1t  | 3g  |
| black pepper   | 1t  | 2g  |
| salt           | 1tb | 18g |

## Steps
- coat with dry mix
- coat with flour
- wings 390gF for 15 mins
- drum sticks 5min extra
