# Pear Bourdaloue

[Matthew Ryle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8owaQbV1ok) · [transcript](../raw/youtube/2026-09-04-pear-bourdaloue-H8owaQbV1ok.md)

23 cm tart ring, perforated, loose bottom. Not blind baked, everything bakes together.

## Poached pears

q | ingredient
--- | ---
4 | Conference or Williams pears, firm but ripe
1 L | water
500 g | caster sugar
1 | vanilla pod, split and scraped
2 | star anise
2 | cinnamon sticks
1 | lemon, juice

- 50% stock syrup. Boil, infuse the spices a couple of minutes before the pears go in.
- Halve, core with a Parisian scoop, pull the fibrous line running to the stalk.
- Cartouche on top plus a weight so nothing pokes out of the liquid, else you get hard and soft patches.
- Barely simmer 20-30 min, until a knife meets no resistance. Cool in the liquid.
- They keep cooking in the oven, so cooked but still holding.

## Pâte sucrée

q | ingredient
--- | ---
250 g | plain flour
125 g | unsalted butter, soft, cubed
85 g | icing sugar, sieved
3 | egg yolks
pinch | fine salt

- Butter **soft**, not cold. This one is creamed, not rubbed in. It comes out like a paste, not a pastry.
- Roll it soft, straight away, between two sheets of baking paper, 2-3 mm, even thickness. Chill hard after. Chilling first then rolling is the mistake.
- Check the size against the tart ring: you need about an inch of overhang all round.
- Grease the mould, peel both papers, drop the disc in centred and confident, work it into the crevices as it softens. Patch tears with offcuts, they disappear.
- Trim so the dough sits a mm or two **above** the rim, it shrinks in the oven.
- Back in the fridge to firm before filling.

## Frangipane

q | ingredient
--- | ---
125 g | unsalted butter, room temperature
125 g | icing sugar, sieved
125 g | ground almonds
2 | large eggs, room temperature (3 if small)
20-25 g | cornflour or plain flour
1 tsp | almond extract
pinch | fine salt

- **Everything at the same temperature**, butter and eggs both out for hours. Cold eggs into warm butter is what splits it.
- Icing sugar in on low speed, then ramp up. Whip pale and creamy a couple of minutes.
- Eggs one at a time, then cornflour, then fold in the almonds.

## Assembly and bake

q | ingredient
--- | ---
50 g | flaked almonds

- Pipe the frangipane in a spiral, only **half way up** the case. Pastry shrinks, frangipane rises.
- Slice the pear halves 5 mm on an angle, thin tip towards the centre of the tart, fan each one as you lay it.
- 6 pears around the tart, one in the middle. Flaked almonds in the gaps and a pinch in the centre.
- 175°C fan, ~40 min, until risen, set through and deep golden.

## Glaze

q | ingredient
--- | ---
150 g | apricot jam, sieved
| icing sugar, to dust

- Any preserve without bits works. Warm it until hot and glossy.
- Brush over the whole top while the tart is still warm.
- Icing sugar on the perimeter only, not across the middle.
- Best at room temperature. Plain crème fraîche.
