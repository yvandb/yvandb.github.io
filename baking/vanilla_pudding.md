# Vanilla Pudding, ultra-soft

[capture](../raw/web/2026-11-22-ultra-soft-vanilla-pudding-pasted.md)

700 g milk version. 3.4% cornstarch to milk. Border between trembling pudding and crème anglaise, past 700 g milk it no longer sets.

q | ingredient
--- | ---
700 g | whole milk
125 g | granulated sugar
24 g | cornstarch
1.5 g | fine salt
50 g | egg yolks (3 large)
28 g | unsalted butter
8 g | pure vanilla extract

- Gentle bubble, whisk a full 60 s. Do not pull early like the 600 g version.
- Cornstarch needs ~95C / 203F to set, else it weeps water later.
- Chill in individual ramekins or glasses, not one big bowl. Too loose to scoop.
- Done: wobbles when you tap the bowl, soft trails when scooped, no sharp edges.
