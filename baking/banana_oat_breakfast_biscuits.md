# Banana Oat Breakfast Biscuits

belVita Soft Baked Banana Bread copycat. 10-12 biscuits.

q | ingr | gr
--- | --- | ---
1 cup | rolled oats, half pulsed coarse half left whole | (90g)
3/4 cup | whole wheat flour | (90g)
1/2 tsp | baking soda | (3g)
1/2 tsp | cinnamon | 
1/4 tsp | salt | 
1 medium | ripe banana, mashed | (80g)
3 tbsp | melted butter or neutral oil | (42g)
3 tbsp | brown sugar or maple syrup | (40g)
2 tbsp | raisin paste | (35g)
1 tbsp | honey or agave | (21g)
1/2 tsp | vanilla | 

- Pulse only half the oats to coarse flour. The other half stays whole, that is where the texture comes from.
- No raisin paste: blend 2 tbsp dark raisins with 1 tbsp warm water. This is what gives the belVita depth, do not skip it.
- Dough comes out thick and tacky. Chill 30 min or they spread and go thin instead of chewy.
- Shape flat ovals, 8mm / 1/3 inch. They barely spread, so shape is final shape.
- 175C / 350F, 12-14 min. Edges set, centers still soft. They firm up while cooling.
- 5 min on the sheet before the rack.
- Airtight 5 days. Frozen, wrapped individually, 2 months.
