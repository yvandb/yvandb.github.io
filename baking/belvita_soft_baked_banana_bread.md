# BelVita Soft Baked Banana Bread

[Gemini chat](https://gemini.google.com/app/3a917586f939c956) · [capture](../raw/web/2026-10-12-belvita-soft-baked-banana-bread-gemini.md)

belVita Soft Baked Banana Bread copycat. 10-12 biscuits.

g | q | ingredient
--- | --- | ---
90 g | 1 cup | rolled oats, half ground coarse, half whole
95 g | 3/4 cup | whole wheat flour
2.5 g | 1/2 tsp | baking soda
1.3 g | 1/2 tsp | cinnamon
1.5 g | 1/4 tsp | salt
150 g | ~3/4 cup | banana, mashed, extra ripe / spotted
55 g | ~1/4 cup packed | brown sugar
45 g | ~3.2 tbsp | butter, melted
25 g | ~1.5 tbsp | honey or agave
30 g | 2 tbsp | raisin paste
2.5 g | 1/2 tsp | vanilla

- Pulse half the oats coarse. Other half whole, for texture.
- Black / heavily spotted bananas. Optional: microwave the mash 30-45 s, concentrates the banana.
- No raisin paste: blend 2 tbsp dark raisins with 1 tbsp warm water.
- Dough thick and tacky. Chill 30 min, do not skip: oats and flour need it to absorb the extra liquid.
- 175C / 350F. Press flat ovals ~1/3 inch. No spread, shape is final shape.
- 10-11 min. Pull as soon as edges set, centers soft.
- 5 min on the sheet, then the rack.
- Airtight 5 days. Frozen, wrapped individually, 2 months.
