# The Ultra-Soft Ratio (700g Milk)

Pasted text, 2026-11-22. No link given.

## Text

```
At that ratio, you are right on the border between a very loose, trembling pudding and a pourable crème anglaise (vanilla custard sauce). If you go much past 700g without adding more starch, it will no longer hold a spoonable "set" and will puddle on a plate.

The "Ultra-Soft" Ratio (700g Milk)
700g whole milk (3.4% cornstarch ratio)

125g granulated sugar (kept proportional so the flavor stays bright)

24g cornstarch

1.5g fine salt

50g egg yolks (3 large yolks)

28g unsalted butter

8g pure vanilla extract

What to Expect & How to Cook It
Texture: Think of a delicate panna cotta or a thick French crème dessert—it will wobble noticeably when you tap the bowl, melt cleanly on the tongue, and leave soft trails rather than sharp edges when scooped.

Don't skip the full 1-minute boil: Unlike the 600g version where you pulled it early, when pushing starch down to ~3.4%, you must bring it to a gentle bubble and whisk for a full 60 seconds. Cornstarch needs to reach full gelatinization temperature (~95°C / 203°F) to build a web strong enough to hold 700g of liquid without weeping water later.

Chill in individual ramekins: A batch this loose holds its delicate structure much better when chilled and served in small individual bowls or glasses rather than scooped out of one large serving bowl.
```
