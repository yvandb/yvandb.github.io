     belVita Soft Baked Banana Bread Biscuits at home, this recipe pairs oats, whole wheat flour,
     mashed banana, and a touch of raisin paste.

     Prep & Bake Specs
             Prep Time: 15 minutes

             Chill Time: 30 minutes

             Bake Time: 12–14 minutes

             Yield: 10–12 soft-baked breakfast biscuits

     Ingredients
     Dry Ingredients:

             1 cup whole rolled oats

             ¾ cup whole wheat flour (or all-purpose flour)

             ½ tsp baking soda

             ½ tsp ground cinnamon

             ¼ tsp salt

     Wet Ingredients:

             1 medium ripe banana, thoroughly mashed (about ⅓ cup)

             3 tbsp melted butter or neutral oil (such as canola or coconut)

             3 tbsp brown sugar or maple syrup

             2 tbsp raisin paste or fine raisin puree (gives the signature depth of belVita soft bakes)

             1 tbsp honey or agave

             ½ tsp vanilla extract

     Instructions

          1. Pulse the Oats: Place ½ cup of the rolled oats into a food processor or blender and pulse
             briefly to break them into coarse oat flakes/flour. Leave the remaining ½ cup oats whole for
             texture.

          2. Mix Dry Ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together the whole wheat flour, both
             processed and whole oats, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt.

          3. Combine Wet Ingredients: In a separate bowl, whisk together the mashed banana, melted
             butter/oil, brown sugar, raisin paste, honey, and vanilla extract until smooth.

          4. Form the Dough: Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir with a spatula until
             completely combined. The dough will be thick and slightly tacky.

          5. Chill: Cover the dough and chill in the refrigerator for 30 minutes. This prevents spreading
             and helps yield a dense, chewy texture.

          6. Shape the Biscuits: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with
             parchment paper. Divide the dough into 10–12 portions. Form each portion into a flat oval or
             rectangular biscuit shape (about ⅓-inch thick).

          7. Bake: Bake for 12 to 14 minutes. The edges should be set, but the centers should remain
             soft.

          8. Cool: Allow the biscuits to cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring them to
             a wire rack to cool completely.

     Kitchen Tips for Best Results
             Raisin Paste Substitute: If you don't have raisin paste, blend 2 tablespoons of dark raisins
             with 1 tablespoon of warm water in a small food processor or mash them thoroughly with a
             fork.

             Storage: Store cooled biscuits in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days,
             or freeze them individually wrapped for up to 2 months.
