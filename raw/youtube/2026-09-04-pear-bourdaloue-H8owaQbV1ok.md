# This is The Dish I Had To Perfect To Become A Chef (Pear Tart)

**Matthew Ryle** · [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8owaQbV1ok) · 12:56

## Description

This is the tart I had to perfect to become a chef. It was my final exam at cooking school, the pear Bourdaloue, I had to make it 40 or 50 times in practice, and now I’ve got it nailed. It's not the most famous French tart, but it might be the most unsung, and one of my favourites.

I'm going to show you how to line the perfect tart mould with a soft sweet pastry that rolls thin and bakes crisp, poach pears in a warmly spiced stock syrup until tender but holding, and make a frangipane that stays pale, fluffy and never splits. Pears fanned on almond cream, baked golden, and the whole thing glazed for a nice shine.

Chef's Notes:
* Roll sweet pastry soft between baking paper, then chill hard — 2-3mm, consistent thickness, patch any tears
* Poaching liquor is 50% stock syrup with vanilla, star anise & cinnamon — use a cartouche to keep pears submerged
* Frangipane must be same temperature — room-temp butter + room-temp eggs, whip butter & sieved icing sugar pale before adding eggs one at a time
* Don't overfill — frangipane halfway up the case, it will rise; pears sliced 5mm on an angle, thin tip to the centre, fanned as you lay them
* Glaze while warm with sieved, hot apricot jam + icing sugar on the perimeter only — serve at room temp with crème fraîche

RECIPE (23cm tart)
Pâte Sucrée:
* 250g plain flour
* 125g unsalted butter, soft (not cold) — cubed
* 85g icing sugar, sieved
* 3 egg yolks
* Pinch of fine salt
Poached Pears:
* 4 Conference or Williams pears, firm but ripe
* 1L water
* 500g caster sugar (50% stock syrup)
* 1 vanilla pod, split and scraped
* 2 star anise
* 2 cinnamon sticks
* Juice of 1 lemon (to keep pears from browning)
Frangipane:
* 125g unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
* 125g icing sugar, sieved (caster sugar also works)
* 125g ground almonds
* 2 large eggs, room temperature (3 if small)
* 20-25g corn flour or plain flour
* 1 tsp almond extract
* Pinch of fine salt
To Finish:
* 50g flaked almonds
* 150g apricot jam, sieved and warmed until glossy
* Icing sugar, to dust the edge

Method:
* Bake at 175°C (fan) for ~40 mins until deep golden and set through
