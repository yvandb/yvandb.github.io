## Steps
- starter overnight at room temp
- mix and knead until soft and smooth
- 90 min rise until nearly doubled
- divide in 3, rough logs, 10 min rest uncovered
- roll each into 24" rope on greased surface
- brush ropes with egg wash, coat generously in sesame seeds
- braid, tuck ends under
- proof 90 min until very puffy
- 425gF 25-35 min until deep golden
