## Ginger Scallion Sauce

| Ingredient  | Amount   | Weight |
|-------------|----------|--------|
| Ginger      | 1/2 inch | 10g    |
| Green onion | 1        | 10g    |
| Neutral oil | 3 tbsp   | 30g    |
| Sesame oil  | 1 tsp    | 3g     |
| Salt        | to taste | 0.5g   |

| Ingredient    | Amount   | Weight |

| Garlic bulbs  | 6        | 10g    |
| Sriracha      | 2 tbsp   | 25g    |
| Sambal        | 2 tbsp   | 25g    |
| Lime juice    | 1 tbsp   | 5g     |
| Chicken broth | 2 tbsp   | 20g    |
| Sugar         | 2 tsp    | 9g     |
